Guide 10·Playlists

How to export a YouTube playlist.

Updated 5 November 2026

Short answer

YouTube has no way to export a playlist. Google Takeout gives you your own playlists, but only as video IDs with no titles. Needle reads the whole playlist, yours or anyone's, and its Export menu copies every link (one per line, ready for NotebookLM), copies titles with links or a Markdown list, or downloads a CSV or JSON file, filtered and sorted however you like.

01·On YouTube

What YouTube gives you

Nothing to export with. Share on a playlist copies one link to the playlist itself, not the videos in it, and copying from the page by hand only gets what's loaded, which is the first 100.

02·Takeout

Google Takeout, for your own playlists

  1. Go to takeout.google.com and press Deselect all.
  2. Tick YouTube and YouTube Music, then All YouTube data included, and leave only playlists ticked.
  3. Export, and wait for the email.

Each playlist arrives as a CSV of video IDs and the time each one was added. No titles, no channels, no lengths, and nothing for playlists other people made. It's a backup, not a list you can read.

03·With Needle

Export it with Needle

  1. Open the playlist, or your Watch Later or Liked videos.
  2. Press the needle button in the bottom right corner, or Alt+Y.
  3. Needle reads every video in it.
  4. Open Export and pick what you need.

04·What you get

What each one gives you

  • Copy links, for NotebookLM. Every video's link, one per line, and nothing else.
  • Copy titles and links. Each title with its channel and length, and the link under it. Good for a message or an email.
  • Copy as a Markdown list. One line per video as a link, for notes apps like Obsidian or Notion.
  • Download CSV. A spreadsheet: title, video ID, link, length, views, how long ago it went up, and views per day. Exported from your history, it adds when you watched each one.
  • Download JSON. Everything Needle read about each video, for code.

05·NotebookLM

Into NotebookLM

NotebookLM takes YouTube videos as sources and works from their captions, so it can only use public videos that have them. A free notebook holds 50 sources, which is why it pays to filter before you copy.

  1. Cut the playlist down to the videos that matter, with the filters below.
  2. Open Export and choose Copy links, for NotebookLM.
  3. In NotebookLM, add a source, choose YouTube, and paste.

06·Filters

Export only what you need

Export takes what's showing, in the order it's showing, so set the sort menu first (Most views, Shortest, Oldest first) and filter in the panel's box:

>10mOnly proper videos, no clips
>100kOnly the ones with over 100K views
unwatchedOnly what you haven't started
@nameOnly one channel's videos

To hand-pick instead, press Select and click the videos you want: the copy options take just those.

Exporting a whole channel works the same way: open the panel on the channel. Searching a channel with Needle covers that.

07·Questions

Questions?

Can I export someone else's playlist?

Yes. Any playlist you can open: public ones, and unlisted ones you have the link to.

Can I export my Watch Later or Liked videos?

Yes, signed in. Needle reads the first 100 of your own lists, the same as the page does. Turn on Deep reading in the popup to export the rest.

How many videos can it export?

As many as it reads: up to the Video limit in the popup, 1,800 by default and up to 10,000.

Does Google Takeout include the video titles?

No. Takeout's playlist files list each video's ID and when it was added, with no titles, and only for playlists you made yourself.

Does my playlist get uploaded anywhere?

No. Needle has no server. The links and files are made in your browser, from what YouTube's own page already loads.
