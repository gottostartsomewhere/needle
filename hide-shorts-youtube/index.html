Guide 06·Shorts

How to hide Shorts on YouTube.

Updated 14 November 2026

Short answer

YouTube has no setting that turns Shorts off on a computer. The only option is Show fewer Shorts in the ⋮ menu on Home's Shorts shelf, which asks for fewer, on Home only. Needle adds a Hide Shorts button beside YouTube's own chips: press it and Shorts are gone from Home, Subscriptions, search and the videos beside the one you're watching. Press it again and they're back.

01·On YouTube

What YouTube gives you

Shorts turn up in three places on a computer: a Shorts shelf on Home, another on your Subscriptions page, and a shelf of them in the middle of search results. YouTube's settings have no switch for any of them.

What there is: the ⋮ menu on Home's Shorts shelf, with a single option, Show fewer Shorts. It's a request to the recommendations, for Home only. Subscriptions and search keep their Shorts whatever you pick there.

02·With Needle

Hide them with Needle

  1. Open YouTube's Home page.
  2. At the end of the row of chips at the top (All, Music, Gaming...) press Hide Shorts. The number on it is how many Shorts that page has.
  3. They're gone, and they stay gone everywhere: Home, Subscriptions, search and beside the video you're watching, until you press it again.

On your Subscriptions page the same button sits next to All subscriptions. You can also switch it on in Needle's toolbar popup, under On YouTube.

03·What counts

What it hides, and what it leaves

  • Hides: the Shorts shelves on Home and Subscriptions, the Shorts shelf in search results, and Shorts in the list beside the video you're watching.
  • Leaves: the Shorts tab in YouTube's left menu, a channel's own Shorts tab, and the Shorts player. Going there is choosing Shorts.

Off by default, like everything Needle changes on YouTube's own pages: nothing disappears until you ask.

04·Combine it

Hide watched videos too

The button beside it, Hide watched, takes the videos you've already finished off Home and Subscriptions, so both pages only show what's new to you. Hiding watched videos covers that one. And if it's one channel's Shorts you're tired of rather than all of them, Mute channel takes that channel out everywhere, Shorts included.

05·Questions

Questions?

Can you turn off Shorts on YouTube?

Not with a setting. On a computer, YouTube only has Show fewer Shorts in the ⋮ menu on Home's Shorts shelf. It doesn't touch Subscriptions or search, and it asks for fewer, not none.

Can I still watch Shorts when I want to?

Yes. The Shorts tab in YouTube's left menu and a channel's own Shorts tab are left alone, since going there is choosing Shorts. And the button turns them back on in one press.

Do Shorts flash up and then disappear?

No. Needle switches the hiding on before YouTube draws the page, so the shelves never show.

Does it work without signing in?

Yes. Hide Shorts needs no account. Hide watched, the button beside it, only shows when you're signed in, since it goes by your watch history.

Does it work on my phone?

No. Needle is for YouTube in a desktop browser: Chrome, or another browser that installs extensions from the Chrome Web Store.

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no paid tier.
