Guide 07·Playlists

How to sort a YouTube playlist by video length.

Updated 5 November 2026

Short answer

YouTube can't sort a playlist by length. Your own playlists and Watch Later sort by date added, date published or popularity, and other people's playlists don't sort at all. Needle reads the whole playlist and sorts it Shortest or Longest first, yours or anyone's, and filters it by minutes, so "what can I watch in the next quarter of an hour" is one click.

01·On YouTube

What YouTube's sort can do

Open your Watch Later, or a playlist you made, and the sort menu at the top offers Manual, Date added (newest), Date added (oldest), Most popular, Date published (newest) and Date published (oldest). Nothing for length. The chips beside it split the list into Videos and Shorts, and that's as close as it gets.

A playlist someone else made has no sort menu at all. It plays in the order its owner set, and the only way to find the short ones is to scroll the whole thing and read every timestamp.

02·With Needle

Sort it with Needle

  1. Open the playlist, or your Watch Later or Liked videos.
  2. Press the needle button in the bottom right corner, or Alt+Y.
  3. Needle reads every video in it, not just the ones on screen.
  4. Open the sort menu and pick Shortest or Longest.

The same menu also sorts by views, upload date and title, and List order puts it back the way the playlist runs.

03·By length

Only the lengths you want

Sorting puts the short ones first. Filtering takes the long ones out altogether. Type into the panel's box:

<10mUnder 10 minutes
>1hOver an hour
>10m <30mBetween 10 and 30 minutes
unwatched <15mShort, and not started yet

Or press + Free time, say how long you have, and only the videos that fit stay.

04·Play in order

Play them in that order

Press Play and what's showing opens as a temporary YouTube playlist, in the order you sorted it, up to 50 videos. Shortest first is a good way through a Watch Later that's grown out of hand: the quick ones go in an evening, and what's left is the stuff worth setting time aside for.

05·Watch Later

From YouTube's search bar

You don't have to open the list at all. Type in:wl sort:shortest into YouTube's search bar and Needle lists your Watch Later shortest first, right in the dropdown. in:wl sort:longest >1h is the other end.

Want the total instead of the order? How long a playlist is, and how much you have left.

06·Questions

Questions?

Can YouTube sort a playlist by duration?

No. The sort menu on your own playlists has Manual, Date added, Most popular and Date published, and playlists made by other people have no sort menu at all.

Does sorting change my playlist on YouTube?

No. Needle only changes the order you see in its panel. The playlist itself, and its order on YouTube, stay exactly as they were.

Can I watch them in that order?

Yes. Play in the panel opens what's showing, in the order shown, as a temporary YouTube playlist of up to 50 videos.

Does it work on Watch Later and Liked videos?

Yes, signed in. Needle reads the first 100 of your own lists, the same as the page does, and the rest too once Deep reading is on in the popup.

Does it work on a channel too?

Yes. The same sort works on any channel's videos, which is the quickest way to find a channel's longest or shortest upload.
