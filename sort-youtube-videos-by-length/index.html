Guide 06·Length

How to sort YouTube videos by length.

Updated 12 October 2026

Short answer

YouTube can't sort by length anywhere. A channel's Videos tab sorts by Latest, Popular or Oldest, and search only has three length buckets. To find the longest video on a channel, open its Videos tab with Needle, a free Chrome extension, and set Sort to Longest. It reads every upload first, so the top of the list really is the longest one.

01·On YouTube

What YouTube lets you sort by

  • A channel's Videos tab: the chips above the grid switch between Latest, Popular and Oldest. No length.
  • Search: Filters has a Duration section with three buckets, under 3 minutes, 3 to 20 minutes and over 20 minutes. That narrows the results, but it doesn't put them in order.
  • Your playlists and Watch Later: the sort menu orders by when videos were added or published, or by popularity. Length isn't one of the options.

02·The gap

Why scrolling doesn't work either

Every thumbnail shows its length, so in theory you could look. In practice a channel's page loads about 30 videos at a time as you scroll, so a channel with 600 uploads means scrolling all of them in and reading every timestamp, and Ctrl+F can't search for "longest".

03·With Needle

Sort any channel by length with Needle

  1. Open the channel's Videos tab.
  2. Press the needle button in the bottom right corner, or Alt+Y.
  3. Needle reads every upload, not only the ones on screen.
  4. Set Sort to Longest. The first video is the longest one on the channel. Shortest works the other way round.

04·Filters

Narrow it to an exact length

Type a length into the panel's box and the sort keeps working on what's left:

>1hLonger than an hour
<10mShorter than 10 minutes
>20m <40mBetween 20 and 40 minutes
unwatched >1hLong videos you haven't started
after:2025Uploaded after 2025, to find the longest recent one

The Length button below the box does the same with presets, and Free time shows only what fits in the minutes you have.

05·Lists and search

Playlists, Watch Later and search

The same panel opens on any playlist, yours or anyone's, and on your Watch Later, with the same Sort. Shortest first on Watch Later is a quick answer to "what can I watch right now".

From YouTube's search bar, add sort:longest or sort:shortest to a search: lofi mix sort:longest puts the results Needle reads in order of length, and in:wl sort:shortest lists your saved videos shortest first.

06·Questions

Questions?

Can YouTube sort a channel by length?

No. A channel's Videos tab sorts by Latest, Popular or Oldest, and that's all. Length isn't a sort option anywhere on YouTube.

Does it count Shorts?

No. Shorts are kept out of the channel list, since YouTube doesn't give them a length to sort on.

How big a channel can it sort?

The whole channel, up to the Video limit in the popup, 1,800 by default, which you can raise. A few hundred videos take a few seconds, then sorting is instant.

Does it work on my phone?

No. Needle is for YouTube in a desktop browser: Chrome, or another browser that installs extensions from the Chrome Web Store.

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no paid tier.
