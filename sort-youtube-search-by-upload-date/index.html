Guide 02·Search

How to sort YouTube search results by upload date.

Updated 5 November 2026

Short answer

YouTube removed Sort by upload date from search in January 2026. Its filters now have Prioritize, with only Relevance and Popularity, and the Upload date filter narrows results to today, this week, this month or this year without putting them in order. Needle brings the sort back: add sort:newest to any search in YouTube's search bar and the results come back newest first.

01·On YouTube

What YouTube's filters do now

Search for something and open Filters. The panel has five groups: Type, Duration, Upload date, Features and Prioritize. Prioritize is what used to be Sort by, and it has two options left, Relevance and Popularity.

Upload date is still there, as Today, This week, This month and This year, but it's a filter, not a sort. Pick This week and you get videos from this week in whatever order YouTube likes, so a video from six days ago can sit above one from this morning. YouTube said the old options weren't working as expected and pointed people to these filters instead. For following news, a launch or a match that just finished, it isn't the same thing.

02·With Needle

Put it back with Needle

  1. Type your search into YouTube's own search bar, and add sort:newest at the end, like iphone 17 review sort:newest.
  2. Press Enter.
  3. Needle opens its panel on the results, newest first: hours ago at the top, then days, then weeks.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and press Load more to read further back.

Already on a results page? Press the needle button in the bottom right corner, or Alt+Y, and pick Newest upload from the sort menu.

03·Combine it

Combine it with other filters

sort:newest date:weekThis week's videos, newest first
sort:newest >10mNewest first, nothing under 10 minutes
sort:newest -@nameNewest first, leaving out one channel
sort:newest >10kNewest first, only ones people are watching
sort:oldestThe oldest first, for the original video on a topic

Every filter Needle understands works here. The search filters guide lists the rest.

04·How exact

How exact the order is

YouTube tells you how old a video is in its own words: 7 hours ago, 3 days ago, 2 years ago. Needle sorts by those. For anything recent that's exact to the hour or the day, which is when sorting by date matters most. Further back, everything YouTube calls 2 years ago counts as the same age, so within that the order is YouTube's.

It also only sorts what YouTube returns. If a video doesn't come up for your words at all, sorting won't find it, so make the search itself specific: the event, the product, the name.

05·On a channel

On a channel, and in Subscriptions

YouTube didn't take sorting off channels. The Videos tab still has Latest and Oldest, and your Subscriptions page lists uploads newest first. Needle's panel works on both too, with length, views and a search box on top. Searching a channel covers that.

06·Questions

Questions?

Did YouTube really remove sort by upload date?

Yes. In January 2026 the Sort by menu became Prioritize, with Relevance and Popularity. Sorting by upload date and by rating went, and so did the Last hour filter. The upload date filters that are left only narrow the results.

How many results does Needle sort?

The first 200 YouTube returns for your search. Load more at the bottom of the panel reads further, and the new ones slot into the order.

Can I sort oldest first?

Yes. Type sort:oldest instead. sort:views puts the most watched first, and sort:longest and sort:shortest go by length.

Does YouTube see sort:newest in my search?

No. Needle takes it out before the search goes to YouTube, so YouTube only ever sees the words you're searching for.

What about a channel's videos?

YouTube still sorts those itself: the Videos tab on a channel has Latest and Oldest. Needle's panel adds length and views to that, and searching inside the channel.
